01 / ABOUT

Stories beyond the obvious.

HARLAN HAWK writes fiction where identity, technology, human relationships, and hidden motives collide. His stories explore what happens when scientific possibility crosses into deeply personal territory—when identity can fracture, familiar truths can become uncertain, and the line between discovery and consequence begins to disappear. The goal is simple: take the reader somewhere unexpected—and leave a question behind.
